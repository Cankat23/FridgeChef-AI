# Serving photograph credits

All photos are bundled locally. They are serving suggestions, not exact photos of AI recipes or proof of allergen absence. Matching checks the recipe name, ingredient groups, preparation words and selected restrictions. Unknown matches use a local serving illustration.

Each image was resized/orientation-corrected and JPEG re-encoded for this package; the browser may crop the display. CC BY-SA images remain under their original share-alike license.
